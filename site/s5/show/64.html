Code of Conduct

Full Code of Conduct: bit.ly/soundconduct

The Sound + Code = Jam event is committed to providing a friendly, safe and welcoming environment for all, regardless of gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion, nationality, or another similar characteristic.

Yes Please

Examples of behaviour that contributes to creating a respectful vibe include:

  • Being kind and courteous to others.
  • Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
  • Showing empathy towards our fellow attendees.
  • Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes.

No Thank You

Examples of unacceptable behavior by event attendees include:

  • The display of inappropriate images or language, including but not limited to sexualized or violent imagery or language.
  • Discriminatory jokes and language.
  • Spamming, flaming, baiting or other attention-stealing behavior.
  • Trolling, insulting, derogatory comments, and personal attacks.
